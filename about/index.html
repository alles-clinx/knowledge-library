Operating principle

The method should match the question.

Use Knowledge to understand, Nova to ask against source material, Metricon to calculate with explicit inputs, and CheckMate to verify whether records agree. None of those modes should silently replace the authoritative source, required measurement or human approval behind a consequential decision.
