AI & Data Use

This page explains how AI-assisted features relate to Alle's ClinX Knowledge, how users should treat AI output, and which data-handling principles apply when a Knowledge page links to an AI service.

Knowledge itself is a static publishing site. AI interactions may occur on separately operated Alle's ClinX services, and the notice shown on that service governs the specific interaction.
01Role of AI

Alle's ClinX may use AI to help users find, understand, or navigate product, safety, compliance, and operational information. AI is used as an assistance layer around source material rather than as a replacement for the source material itself.

02Source hierarchy

For product-specific or safety-critical questions, current official documents take priority. The product label, Safety Data Sheet, Technical Data Sheet, approved operating procedure, and applicable legal requirement should be checked before acting on an AI-generated answer.

Where an AI answer cannot be supported by the available source material, the safer outcome is to identify that limitation rather than invent a fact.

03User inputs

Do not submit personal, confidential, medical, financial, credential, or customer information unless it is genuinely required by the service you are using and the service notice explains how it will be handled.

For ordinary Knowledge questions, keep inputs limited to the product, task, surface, document, or operational context needed to answer the question.

04AI output and human judgment

AI output can be incomplete, outdated, or incorrect. Users remain responsible for checking consequential instructions against current source documents and for applying appropriate professional, supervisory, or site-specific judgment.

AI output should not be treated as a regulatory approval, certification, laboratory result, or legal determination unless an identified authoritative source actually provides that status.

05Calculations and structured tools

Where a calculation affects dosing, concentration, procurement, or another operational decision, the input values and units should be checked before relying on the result. A calculator or AI explanation does not correct an inaccurate input automatically.

06Data handling

Data handling for an AI interaction follows the privacy notice and product-specific notice applicable to the service where the interaction occurs. This page does not make a broader claim about model training, retention, or third-party processing beyond those published notices.

07Feedback and corrections

If an AI-assisted answer appears inconsistent with an official Alle's ClinX document, report the issue with the relevant product or document name so the discrepancy can be reviewed against the source.

08Changes

We may update this page as AI features, source systems, data practices, or applicable requirements change. Material changes will be reflected in the version date below.
