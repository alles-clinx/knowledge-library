# Translation Standard

- Translate only from the canonical English source.
- One language is visible at a time in the website UI.
- Use a minimal language switch such as `English | हिन्दी`.
- Keep canonical article identity shared across locales.
- Technical terms may remain in English or appear in parentheses when that improves clarity.
- Translation must preserve meaning, cautions, references and content hierarchy.
- Hindi translation records remain `translation-review` until reviewed.
