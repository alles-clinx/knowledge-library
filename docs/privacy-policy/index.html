Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the Alle's ClinX Knowledge website handles information when you browse, search, or install the site as a progressive web app.

This page applies to the GitHub-hosted Knowledge site. Forms, accounts, orders, payments, and commercial interactions on allesclinx.com are covered by the privacy notices published there.
01Information you provide

The Knowledge website does not require an account, checkout, or payment information to read its published guides. If you follow a link to email Alle's ClinX or use a form on another Alle's ClinX service, the information you submit is handled under the notice that applies to that service.

02Technical information

Like other websites, page delivery can involve technical information such as an IP address, browser and device information, requested URL, timestamps, and network or security logs. Some of this information may be processed by GitHub or other infrastructure providers as part of hosting, delivery, abuse prevention, and reliability.

03Knowledge search

The Knowledge search interface loads a static search index and ranks matches in your browser. Search terms entered into the on-site Knowledge search are not sent by the site's own search code to a separate Alle's ClinX search server.

Following a result simply navigates to the selected published page.

04Progressive web app and offline cache

The site uses a service worker so supported browsers can cache site assets and previously visited content for faster or offline access. Browser Cache Storage is local to your device and is not the same as an advertising cookie.

You can remove cached site data through your browser or device settings.

05External services and links

Links from Knowledge may open allesclinx.com, GitHub, email, or other services. Those services may receive information according to their own technical operation and privacy notices. Their data practices are not controlled by this static Knowledge page.

06How information is used

Information associated with hosting or support may be used to deliver pages, maintain security and availability, investigate faults or abuse, respond to enquiries, and meet legal obligations. Alle's ClinX does not use this page to authorise sale of personal data.

07Retention and your choices

Retention periods depend on the system involved. Local browser caches remain on your device until they expire or you remove them. Hosting-provider logs and any support correspondence are retained according to the provider or business process that created them.

You can clear browser site data, avoid optional external links, or contact Alle's ClinX about a privacy request.

08Privacy requests and changes

For privacy questions, correction requests, or other data-rights enquiries, contact Alle's ClinX using the address below. We may update this page when the Knowledge architecture, applicable law, or connected services change.
