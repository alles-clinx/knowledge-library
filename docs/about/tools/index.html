About Alle's ClinX Digital Tools

The Alle's ClinX digital layer separates three different jobs: answering questions from source material, running repeatable calculations, and checking whether records agree. Nova, Metricon and CheckMate are designed around those distinct jobs rather than forcing them into one generic interface.

About NovaAbout MetriconAbout CheckMate

Three jobs

Ask. Calculate. Verify.

Each tool has a narrower operating role so users can choose the right method for the question in front of them.

Nova · ask

Use Nova when the question depends on Alle's ClinX product information, safety documentation, certifications, policies or other approved source material.

Understand Nova

Metricon · calculate

Use Metricon when the job is numerical: procurement, usage, dilution, coverage, chemistry, concentration or risk calculations.

Understand Metricon

CheckMate · verify

Use CheckMate when several records should agree and you need to expose missing evidence, contradictions or approval gaps.

Understand CheckMate

Operating model

The tool should match the evidence.

A question from a document, a mathematical calculation and a cross-record verification problem are different kinds of work. The suite keeps those modes explicit.

Source-grounded answers

Nova retrieves from the Alle's ClinX information corpus rather than relying on general web knowledge for product and compliance answers.

Deterministic formulas

Metricon uses defined calculation models so users can inspect inputs, units and assumptions rather than accept a conversational estimate.

Explainable checks

CheckMate keeps the reason for each flag visible so a reviewer can see which records conflict or what evidence is still missing.
