About Alle's ClinX Knowledge

Alle's ClinX Knowledge is the open, structured reference layer for professional cleaning, hygiene, safety, chemistry, equipment and facility operations. It is designed to make practical guidance easier to find, verify, reuse and apply.

Browse KnowledgeExplore digital tools

Purpose

A reference system, not a marketing catalogue.

Knowledge is organised around operational questions and repeatable tasks. The aim is to explain the why, what and how behind professional hygiene work while keeping product-specific instructions connected to their authoritative documents.

Learn the principle

Use the articles to understand concepts such as cleaning chemistry, disinfection, surface care, routines, equipment and facility planning.

Find the source

Safety-critical and product-specific decisions should be checked against the current product label, SDS, TDS, SOP or other authoritative source.

Move from reading to action

Knowledge connects guidance to related tools such as Nova for document-grounded questions, Metricon for calculations and CheckMate for evidence verification.

Structure

Built as a layered library.

The library uses a consistent category → topic → article hierarchy so a user can move from a broad operational area to a specific answer without learning a new navigation model on every page.

Categories

Large operating areas such as Cleaning & Housekeeping, Cleaning Chemistry, Safety & Compliance and Facility Guides.

Topics

Focused subcategories that narrow the question to a practical domain or workflow.

Articles

Individual guides with a stable article ID, references, related material and bilingual support where published.

Open knowledge

Readable, reusable and attributable.

Knowledge article content is published under an open-content approach so practical guidance can be read, downloaded, shared and reused with attribution, subject to the licence shown on the relevant page.

CC BY 4.0

Original Knowledge article content is generally released under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 unless a page says otherwise.

Read licensing terms

Brand remains separate

The open-content licence does not automatically license Alle's ClinX trademarks, logos, product trade dress or third-party material.

One language at a time

Where English and Hindi versions exist, the interface presents a single language view and a clear language switch to preserve reading order.

Trust

Source-first by design.

Knowledge is useful only when users can understand where a claim comes from and when a general guide stops being enough.

References

Technical claims are supported by source references where the article format calls for them.

Corrections

Pages can be corrected or updated as product documentation, standards or evidence changes.

Clear boundaries

General guidance does not replace current product documents, legal requirements or qualified professional judgment where those are required.
